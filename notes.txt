partial derivative = the rate at which a function changes with respect to one of its variables

chain rule= a way to compute gradients of a loss function given the model's parameters

grad_L_w1= grad(loss,w1,retain_graph=True) #partial derivative of the loss with respect to w1 (how much does the loss change
                                          # and in what direction if I change this weight )

BERT= bidirectional encoder representations from transformers
GPT= generative pretrained transformers
BPE = byte pair encoding

attention score= dot product of the embedded vector of a word with the vector of all the words in the context

dot product= beyond the mathematical operation, the dot product of 2 vectors represents the similarity of the 2 vectors,
                meaning how closely the vectors are aligned. In the context of self-attention, the dot product represents
                to what extent each element in the sequence focuses on any other element. The
                higher the dot product, the higher the similarity and attention score between two
                elements.
